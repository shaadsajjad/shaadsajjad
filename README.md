## 🚀 Selected Works

### 🔹 Shiftick: Attendance Tracker · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.invalidco.officehour.office_hour&hl=en)
Employee attendance and work-time management app. **I designed and built the whole thing solo**, from the architecture, UI and backend structure through to the Play Store release.
- **Shift-setup onboarding:** multi-step flow plus role-based employee management
- **Weekly roster & scheduling:** per-employee schedules, day-off handling, automatic shift-time resolution
- **Team page:** shift assignment for the whole team
- **Stack:** Flutter · PocketBase · go_router · EasyLocalization (multi-language)

### 🔹 Epifin Coffee: POS System
Live Point-of-Sale app running on Sunmi devices (Flutter · BLoC/Cubit · clean architecture).
- **Split payments:** one order settled across multiple payment methods
- **Reactive discount engine:** totals recalculate automatically as items and discounts change
- **Order caching & background refresh** keep the counter fast
- **Receipt printing** on Sunmi hardware

### 🔹 Appuly · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.invalidco.appuly.appuly&hl=en) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/appuly-put-screenshot-in-frame/id1636138219)
Puts app screenshots in realistic device frames for store listings. I built device-frame selection, background customization and the export flow.

### 🔹 Ansal: Black & White Photos · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.invalidco.bnw_gallery&hl=en) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tn/app/black-and-white-filter-ansal/id1609664136)
